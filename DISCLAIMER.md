# US CRE Loan Maturity & Risk Dataset — Source & Liability Disclaimer

**Publisher:** IT Altitudes Inc.

**Primary Source:** U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) — EDGAR filings

**Geographic Coverage:** United States

## 1. Intended Purpose

This dataset is a static reference resource prepared for commercial real estate research, loan maturity analysis, credit risk research, portfolio analysis, and non-production testing.

It is not a live loan-servicing, credit-monitoring, or financial reporting system.

## 2. Source Information

The dataset contains structured reference information curated from publicly available SEC EDGAR filings.

The original filing information may include loan-level disclosures, property information, reported financial characteristics, and related references.

IT Altitudes Inc. is an independent dataset curator and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the SEC.

## 3. Loan-Level Interpretation

- Records represent source-reported loan interests and should not automatically be interpreted as distinct underlying whole mortgages.
- Reported loan amounts, balances, rates, and maturity dates may reflect the applicable filing's reporting period.
- Information may be amended, updated, or superseded.
- Different filings may reference related loans or underlying properties.

Users should verify material information against original SEC filings.
